==============================================================================
SECTION 6. Why degenerate responders were excluded from the DDM
==============================================================================
The stDDM estimates harm and cure sensitivity from the *contrast* between
Recommend and Do-Not-Recommend responses and their RT distributions. A
participant who gives one response on (nearly) every trial provides no
information about the boundary they never reach: the drift coefficients
and the relative-onset parameter are only weakly identified, and their
posteriors collapse onto the group prior. Including them would not recover
their preferences -- it would import shrinkage estimates that look like
group means and would attenuate exactly the individual-difference effects
the paper is about. The robustness analysis below therefore uses a
model-free sensitivity measure that *is* estimable for these participants.

==============================================================================
SECTION 8. Caveats
==============================================================================
1. Trait comparisons cover GRiPS, Berlin and subjective numeracy, the five
   BFI-2 domains, religiosity, spirituality and age. These are the measures
   whose scoring keys could be verified item-for-item against the published
   composites, so excluded and retained participants are strictly comparable.
   Moral Foundations and Moral Commitments composites were built in an
   upstream pipeline not contained in this repository and are available only
   for the eligible pool, so they enter the section 7 robustness check but
   not the section 5 comparison against the prior-study exclusions.
2. The reviewer also raises decision-making responsibility toward others.
   No responsibility measure was administered, so that specific claim cannot
   be tested here and should be conceded as a limitation.
3. The BFI-2 domain columns originally distributed with this project were
   unreversed item means (verified against the pre-fix backup: exact
   match to rowMeans over unreversed items, mean inter-domain r = +.68).
   fix_bfi2_composites.R re-scored them on 2026-09-10, so section 5 uses
   the corrected keying while the manuscript reports the unreversed ones.
   No reported result changes: the composites never enter a published
   analysis, and the item-level ridge importances are invariant to
   reverse-scoring (max difference 4e-16). The exclusion comparison in
   section 5 holds under either scoring.
4. The section 7 proxy is a static logistic model. It recovers sensitivity
   to harm and cure but has no analogue of the relative-onset (time)
   parameter, so it can show that the magnitude effects are not an artifact
   of the exclusion, but it cannot do the same for the latency effects.
